About Us

Didi Quail Farm is a specialized quail farm delivering fresh quails, quail eggs, day-old chicks, and breeding pairs across Kasama District. Our delivery agents ensure your birds arrive healthy and on time.

🐦 Why Quails?

• High protein, low cholesterol meat
• Nutritious eggs (rich in vitamins)
• Fast-growing and low-cost farming
• Great for small-scale farmers
• Medicinal value in traditional health
